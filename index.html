Before you install

Questions.

What does Fast Skim actually do?

It bolds the first few letters of every word, so your eye has a fixed point to land on instead of drifting back over the line. Nothing but the type changes. Turn it on or off per book, whenever you want.

Which files can I read?

EPUB files you already have. Import one from your device and it lands in your library. There is no store to buy from and no DRM server to check in with.

Is it really free?

Yes. No price, no ads, no subscription, and nothing locked behind a paid tier.

What happens offline?

Everything except two things. Reading, highlights, bookmarks, themes and stats all work with the SIM out. Only the dictionary lookup and web search need a connection, and only when you tap them.

Where do my books and highlights live?

On your phone. There is no account to make and nothing syncs anywhere. Uninstall the app and it all goes with it.
